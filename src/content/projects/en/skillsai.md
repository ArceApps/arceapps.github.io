SkillsAI is a personal collection that I maintain for my ArceApps and SpecAI projects. It includes original skills and adaptations for my SpecAI and mobile-development workflows, and each one documents why it is useful, its source, author and licence.

The repository includes the public [Android Skills catalogue](https://github.com/android/skills) under Apache-2.0, plus attributed MIT sources such as [karpathy-llm-wiki](https://github.com/Astro-Han/karpathy-llm-wiki) and [ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail). The personal collection and its adaptations can be used freely while respecting each licence. See the [full credits and provenance register](https://github.com/ArceApps/skillsAI/blob/main/CREDITS.md).
